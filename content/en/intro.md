*The Thirty-Seven Practices of a Bodhisattva* was composed by the Tibetan master **Ngulchu Thogme Zangpo** (1295–1369). It is a clear, concise guide to living for the benefit of all beings.

Each practice is a step on the path — from cherishing this human life to dedicating merit. Read slowly, and return to the lines that speak to you.

This app is a quiet offline reader. Nothing extra — just the text.
